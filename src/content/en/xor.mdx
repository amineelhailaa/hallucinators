## How it works

XOR assigns one to inputs that differ and zero to inputs that match. A single affine boundary cannot separate the diagonal pairs. Hidden ReLU units can create two half-space features whose combination separates them. This is a small explicit example of feature construction, not a claim that training always finds these exact weights.

## Worked example

h₁=ReLU(x₁−x₂), h₂=ReLU(x₂−x₁); y=h₁+h₂. On binary inputs, outputs are 0,1,1,0.

## Common misconception

The hidden units are learned numerical features, not necessarily named human concepts.
