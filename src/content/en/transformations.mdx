## How it works

Invariance means the output stays the same under a transformation; equivariance means it changes in a predictable corresponding way. Shifting a circle should usually preserve its shape label. Rotating an up arrow clockwise changes its direction to right. For position regression, shifting the object should change its coordinates. Augmentation must respect the label semantics.

## Worked example

Mirroring [[1,2],[3,4]] changes the flat vector to [2,1,4,3]. A shared convolution can still respond, but its maps and dense outputs may change.

## Common misconception

Pooling and shared kernels do not guarantee invariance to every shift, flip or rotation.
