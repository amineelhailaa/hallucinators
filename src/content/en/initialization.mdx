## How it works

Parameters are trainable values; hyperparameters configure learning. Random initialization breaks symmetry between units. Identically initialized hidden units can receive identical updates and remain redundant. Glorot and He initialization scale randomness with layer dimensions. More parameters increase capacity but can also encourage overfitting.

## Worked example

Dense(16) after 128 features has 128×16+16=2,064 parameters. Batch size is a hyperparameter and adds no weights.

## Common misconception

A random seed improves reproducibility but does not promise bit-identical results across every platform.
