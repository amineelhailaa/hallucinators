## How it works

Training fit and unseen-data performance are different. Underfitting means the model fails to capture useful structure; overfitting means it adapts too much to training-specific details. Compare training and validation curves. Weight decay, appropriate augmentation, dropout, and early stopping can help, but none guarantees generalization.

## Worked example

Training loss falling from 0.5 to 0.1 while validation rises from 0.6 to 0.9 suggests growing overfit. Select checkpoints using validation.

## Common misconception

A single noisy validation epoch is not enough to prove a trend. Inspect multiple epochs and data quality.
