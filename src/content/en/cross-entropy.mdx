## How it works

Categorical cross-entropy penalizes low probability assigned to the correct class. One-hot targets work with categorical loss; integer class IDs work with sparse categorical loss. Binary cross-entropy matches binary or independent multi-label targets. A logits-aware loss computes a stable combination without applying softmax twice.

## Worked example

Correct-class probability 0.8 gives −ln(0.8)=0.223; 0.01 gives 4.605. Confidently wrong predictions cost much more.

## Common misconception

Cross-entropy is not calculated on an argmax class ID. It needs the score distribution or logits.
