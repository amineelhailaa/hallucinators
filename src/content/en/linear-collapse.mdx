## How it works

Composing affine functions changes their coefficients but not their family. Ten affine layers still produce one affine transformation. A nonlinear activation prevents this general collapse. Depth can then reuse intermediate representations to form more expressive functions, although depth alone does not guarantee better learning.

## Worked example

f₁(x)=2x+1 and f₂(h)=3h−4 compose to 6x−1. Adding ReLU between them introduces a bend at x=−0.5.

## Common misconception

More layers without nonlinearities do not create arbitrary nonlinear boundaries.
