## How it works

Hidden representations transform the coordinates in which a task is solved. A difficult boundary in input space may become simple in feature space. Inspect the four XOR points before and after the hidden layer. Visualizations reveal values and patterns but do not establish a unique explanation of the model’s reasoning.

## Worked example

XOR points (0,0) and (1,1) map to (0,0); (1,0) maps to (1,0), and (0,1) to (0,1). The sum of hidden coordinates separates their labels.

## Common misconception

Discarding a distinction between different labels can make the task impossible for later layers.
