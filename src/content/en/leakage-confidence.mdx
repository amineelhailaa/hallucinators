## How it works

A model can exploit shortcuts unrelated to the intended task. Duplicates across splits inflate evaluation. Distribution shift changes the relationship between training and deployment examples. Softmax normalizes available scores even for unfamiliar inputs, so a high score is not evidence that the input belongs to a known class. Evaluate failures and data provenance.

## Worked example

A four-class model must still assign four scores to a blank image. Scores summing to one do not mean the image is a valid arrow.

## Common misconception

Do not describe attention or feature brightness as proof of causal reasoning.
