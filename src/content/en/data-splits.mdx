## How it works

Training data drives parameter updates. Validation guides configuration and model selection. Test estimates performance after choices are frozen. Related or duplicate examples must not cross splits. Split before augmentation, and preserve groups such as subjects where applicable. Repeatedly choosing settings from test results turns that test into validation.
