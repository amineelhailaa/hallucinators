## How it works

Convolution itself is affine when bias is included. ReLU applies independently to every output position and channel. The two operations have different jobs: convolution combines inputs; activation creates nonlinearity. Intermediate feature maps retain a spatial grid. Inspect their numeric values before interpreting bright areas as meaningful features.

## Worked example

A convolution output [−3,2,0,5] becomes [0,2,0,5] through ReLU without changing its shape.

## Common misconception

Convolution alone does not supply the nonlinear expressive power of a deep CNN.
