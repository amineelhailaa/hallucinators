## How it works

Logits are unconstrained output scores. Softmax converts several logits into positive values summing to one for mutually exclusive classes. Sigmoid processes each logit independently and fits binary or multi-label tasks. Argmax selects a class but discards score information. Subtract the maximum logit before exponentiation to avoid overflow.

## Worked example

Logits [0,0,0,0] yield four probabilities of 0.25. Adding 100 to every logit leaves these probabilities unchanged.

## Common misconception

A score near one can still be wrong or poorly calibrated, especially outside the training distribution.
