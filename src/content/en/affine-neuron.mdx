## How it works

A dot product measures an input along a weighted direction. With bias, the neuron defines an affine function. In two dimensions, a fixed output corresponds to a line. Changing the weights rotates or scales that line; changing the bias translates it. “Linear layer” is common terminology even though a layer with bias is mathematically affine.

## Worked example

z=2x₁+x₂−3 has z=0 at (1,1) and (0,3). Both points lie on its boundary.

## Common misconception

An affine boundary cannot solve every classification problem; XOR is a counterexample.
