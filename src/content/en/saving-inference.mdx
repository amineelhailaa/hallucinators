## How it works

Save topology, weights, label order and preprocessing together. Reload and compare predictions against the original model. Inference uses frozen parameters and evaluation mode; it needs no target. Transfer learning reuses learned representations, often training a new head before carefully unfreezing selected layers with a smaller learning rate. Recompile after changing trainable flags in Keras.

## Worked example

The same image must produce matching scores before and after reload within a stated numerical tolerance. Check all weights remain unchanged after prediction.

## Common misconception

predict does not train. A wrong prediction does not update the model unless a separate learning step is requested.
