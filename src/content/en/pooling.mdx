## How it works

Max pooling keeps the largest value in each window. Average pooling keeps the mean. Both reduce spatial detail and normally have no trainable weights. Later units see larger regions of the original image because preceding windows combine information. Pooling can improve tolerance to some local shifts but can also erase task-relevant distinctions.

## Worked example

Windows [1,0,0,0] and [0,1,0,0] both max-pool to 1 and average-pool to 0.25. Their within-window positions are lost.

## Common misconception

Pooling is not invertible in general. Flatten is a reshape, but pooling discards values.
