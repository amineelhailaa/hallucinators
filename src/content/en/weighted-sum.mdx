## How it works

A neuron first multiplies each input by a weight and adds the products. Bias adds an offset independent of the input. Positive and negative weights can amplify or oppose an input’s contribution. Weights are parameters, not the incoming pixel values. Training changes the parameters while a forward pass calculates outputs from their current values.

## Worked example

For x=[2,3], w=[0.5,-1], b=2, the sum is 1−3+2=0.

## Common misconception

A large input is not automatically important: its contribution also depends on its weight.
