## How it works

Activation transforms the weighted sum into the neuron’s output. The local derivative tells us how a small change in the sum affects that output. Saturated sigmoid outputs change very little even when the sum changes. This derivative becomes a factor in backpropagation. Activation is a numerical function, not a biological on/off switch.

## Worked example

For sigmoid, z=0 gives a=0.5 and derivative 0.25. At z=8, a≈0.9997 and derivative≈0.00034.

## Common misconception

Sigmoid values are not automatically calibrated probabilities. Their interpretation depends on training and evaluation.
